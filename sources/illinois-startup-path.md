# Illinois startup-path source notes

Last checked: 2026-08-17

These notes document the primary/authoritative basis used when drafting the Illinois acquisition page and Startup Path screening tool. They are not customer-facing legal advice.

## IRS — Form 1023-EZ
- https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez
- The IRS requires completion of the Form 1023-EZ Eligibility Worksheet before using Form 1023-EZ.
- A “Yes” answer to any worksheet question makes the organization ineligible for Form 1023-EZ and directs it to Form 1023.
- Initial worksheet financial screens include projected/past annual gross receipts over $50,000 and total assets over $250,000.
- The worksheet contains many additional organizational/structural eligibility questions; the website assessment therefore deliberately does **not** declare a user eligible.

## IRS — application overview
- https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/how-to-apply-for-501c3-status
- Organizations applying for recognition use Form 1023 or, if eligible, Form 1023-EZ, submitted through Pay.gov with the applicable user fee.

## Illinois Legal Aid Online — starting a nonprofit
- https://www.illinoislegalaid.org/legal-information/start-nonprofit-organization
- Illinois nonprofit corporations have a board of at least three directors.
- Startup topics include charitable purpose, registered agent/office, incorporation, bylaws, board documentation, additional state/federal filings, and possible Illinois Attorney General charitable registration.

## Illinois Legal Aid Online — articles
- https://www.illinoislegalaid.org/legal-information/articles-incorporation-non-profit-organization
- Illinois articles require, among other information, the registered agent/office, at least three initial directors, and organizational purpose.

## Product guardrail
The Startup Path tool is an educational triage/roadmap. It must not say a user **is eligible** for 1023-EZ based only on the simplified questions. It should direct users to the current official IRS Eligibility Worksheet before filing.
