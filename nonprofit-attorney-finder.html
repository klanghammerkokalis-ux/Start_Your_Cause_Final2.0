What to ask before booking

  • Have you handled this kind of work for a nonprofit like ours?
  • What does the first consultation cost, and what will it cover?
  • Can you review our draft documents? What would a fixed-fee review include?

Check the attorney’s current license status with the applicable licensing authority. Start Your Cause provides search links without endorsing individual attorneys. Availability, eligibility, and fees are determined by each provider.
