# First 10 Customer Outreach

## Founder/community post
Thinking about starting a nonprofit but not sure whether you are actually ready to file?

It may tell you to move forward. It may tell you to do more groundwork first. Either answer is useful before you spend money on filing fees.

Free assessment: /nonprofit-readiness.html

## Early-customer interview questions
1. What made you decide to start a nonprofit now?
2. What part of the process feels most confusing?
3. What have you already tried or researched?
4. What would make you trust an online formation guide?
5. What would stop you from paying for one?
6. If Start Your Cause saved you several hours of research and gave you the documents/roadmap you needed, what would feel like a reasonable price?

## Rule
Do not promise legal representation, guaranteed IRS approval, guaranteed timelines, or individualized legal/tax advice.
