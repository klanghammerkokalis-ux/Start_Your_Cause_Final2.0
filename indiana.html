The Indiana path, in plain language

1
Define your charitable purpose.
Clarify your mission, intended activities, and the people or community you expect to serve.
2
Choose your governance structure and initial leadership.
Decide who will govern the organization and establish the operating framework you will document in your bylaws.
3
Choose your corporate name and required filing information.
Prepare the principal-office and other organizational information required for Indiana formation.
4
File Indiana nonprofit Articles of Incorporation.
Indiana uses State Form 4162 for a domestic nonprofit corporation. The form specifically notes that organizations seeking federal exemption need appropriate IRS language in their purpose article.
5
Adopt bylaws and document organizational action.
Put your governance rules in writing and preserve the approvals and records establishing how the organization will operate.
6
Get an EIN and determine your IRS application path.
Organizations seeking 501(c)(3) recognition generally use Form 1023 or, when eligible, Form 1023-EZ.
7
Handle Indiana nonprofit tax registration.
Indiana DOR says organizations seeking nonprofit sales-tax exemption must have IRS recognition and submit Form NP-20A through INTIME. Indiana also requires periodic nonprofit reporting to maintain state registration.
