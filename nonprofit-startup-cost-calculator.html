What this estimate includes

The total combines the incorporation fee shown in the Start Your Cause state guide, the selected IRS user fee, any charitable-solicitation registration fee listed in the guide, your optional support amount, and your selected buffer. Incorporation does not automatically create federal tax-exempt status.
