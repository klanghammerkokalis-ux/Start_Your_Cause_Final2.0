What the $149 pilot includes

  1. A short written intake. Tell us your state, your idea, and the steps you have already completed.

Email a brief description of where you are stuck. We confirm whether the request fits this educational planning service, what is included, and a delivery date. If you choose to proceed, payment instructions are provided after that confirmation. Your roadmap is delivered by email.
