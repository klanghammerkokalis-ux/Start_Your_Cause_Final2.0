Form 1023 or Form 1023-EZ?

Form 1023-EZ is available only to certain organizations. This short tool identifies obvious indicators that point toward the full Form 1023, but it does not replace the official IRS eligibility worksheet.

Do not rely only on the thresholds

Being small enough financially does not automatically make an organization eligible. Entity type, organizational history, activities, classification, and other facts can require the full application. Complete the IRS worksheet in the current Form 1023-EZ instructions immediately before filing.

Service boundary: Start Your Cause generates customizable templates and educational guidance. We do not submit filings or government fees to a state agency or the IRS. Review current agency instructions before filing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $50,000 test the only 1023-EZ requirement?

No. The IRS also limits eligibility based on assets, entity type, organizational history, classification, and activities. Complete the official worksheet.

Can a church use Form 1023-EZ?

Churches and certain related organizations have special rules and should review the current IRS instructions carefully. The preliminary screener treats this as a full-review indicator.

Where are Form 1023 applications submitted?

The IRS requires Form 1023-series applications to be submitted electronically through Pay.gov.

Does this screener determine eligibility?

No. It identifies obvious risk indicators and directs users to the current official IRS eligibility worksheet, which controls.
