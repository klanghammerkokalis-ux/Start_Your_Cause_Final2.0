# Pricing Experiment Guardrails

Current launch principle: do not change the existing paid plans merely because competitors charge more. First prove conversion with the current product.

Market reference (August 2026): nonprofit formation/501(c)(3) assistance spans inexpensive state-formation offers through $249 DIY software, roughly $499 guided products, and $999-$1,499+ hands-on services. Government filing fees remain separate in many offers.

## Experiment after first customers
Test a higher-value guided tier only after the self-service funnel has real usage data.

Possible tier concept (requires scope/legal review before launch):
- self-service: existing plan
- guided setup: product walkthrough + document consistency checklist + formation roadmap

Do not describe the guided tier as attorney review, legal advice, tax advice, filing representation, or guaranteed approval unless qualified professionals/services are actually contracted to provide those things.

## Decision rule
Raise/expand pricing based on conversion, support burden and willingness-to-pay evidence—not competitor price alone.
