What belongs in nonprofit bylaws?

Bylaws are the board’s operating rules. A useful template must be customized to match the organization, state law, and the way the board will actually work.

Core sections

  • Name and purposes aligned with the Articles and intended exempt purposes.
  • Board structure including size, eligibility, terms, election, removal, and vacancies.
  • Meetings and voting including notice, quorum, remote participation, and written consent.
  • Officers including duties, selection, terms, and removal.
  • Committees and records defining delegated authority and required documentation.
  • Conflicts and amendments explaining recusals and how the bylaws may change.

Bylaws vs. Articles

ArticlesBylaws
Creates the corporation with the stateGoverns internal operations
Usually filed publiclyUsually kept in corporate records
Contains required legal provisionsContains detailed board procedures
Adopt them formally. The initial board should review and approve the bylaws, record that approval in organizational minutes, and retain the signed or final version.
