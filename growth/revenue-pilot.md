# Revenue pilot: Guided Start and DIY formation

## Offer and fulfillment

Existing DIY: $79 for 12 months; existing FOUNDERS49 introductory code, limited to 10 first-time customers. Do not reset that limit or invent an offer expiry.

Guided Start pilot: $149 for written educational planning, sold by inquiry. No document package included. No call required. Deliver one startup task roadmap, official resource links, an information checklist, and one consolidated follow-up email exchange within 14 days of delivery. Confirm scope, availability, price, delivery date, and cancellation/refund terms in writing before taking payment. Do not request payment until an actual supported payment method is ready. Do not collect card information by email.

Accept only requests within administrative organization and educational support. Exclude legal/tax advice, legal drafting or review, exemption eligibility decisions, application preparation/submission, and representation. Refer professional questions to qualified advisers.

Fulfillment checklist:
1. Read inquiry and confirm fit without asking for sensitive records.
2. Estimate work; target no more than 90 minutes total per client for this pilot. Decline or re-scope requests that will exceed the limit.
3. Confirm scope and delivery date; provide terms and secure payment instructions.
4. Verify payment before beginning paid work.
5. Prepare a roadmap using reported progress, distinguishing confirmed facts from unknowns. Link official instructions rather than making unsupported eligibility judgments.
6. Deliver roadmap and explain the follow-up window.
7. Record actual work time, revenue, fees, refunds, and customer feedback. Ask permission before using any testimonial.

## Revenue arithmetic, not a forecast

$3,000 gross sales requires 38 sales at $79, 62 sales at $49, or 21 Guided Start sales at $149. A mixed example: 10 Guided Start sales ($1,490) + 20 regular DIY sales ($1,580) = $3,070 gross. Fees, refunds, taxes, and labor reduce take-home income. At the 90-minute fulfillment target, 10 Guided Start clients require up to 15 hours of delivery work, excluding marketing.

First validation target: three paid customers, with the acquisition source recorded for each. Do not treat inquiries or email clicks as purchases.
