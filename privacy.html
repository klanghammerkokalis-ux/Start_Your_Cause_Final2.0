Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Start Your Cause is designed so that questionnaire answers and generated documents remain in your browser. Payment processing is handled by Stripe.

Information handled by the service

Your questionnaire answers are saved in your browser's local storage so you can return to your work on the same browser and device. Document generation occurs in your browser. Start Your Cause does not intentionally send questionnaire answers or generated document contents to its servers.

If you email us, we receive the information you include in that message. Do not email sensitive personal, financial, medical, or authentication information.

Payments and subscription verification

Stripe processes subscription payments. Start Your Cause does not receive or store your full card number. After checkout, the service sends the Stripe Checkout Session reference to a server function so Stripe can confirm payment and active subscription status. Stripe's own privacy terms govern the information Stripe processes.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, questionnaire progress, pricing views, checkout activity, and purchases. Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and may receive browser, device, and general usage information. Questionnaire answers and generated document contents are not intentionally included in analytics events.

Browser storage

The service uses local or session storage for questionnaire progress, checklist progress, analytics duplicate protection, and the Stripe Checkout Session reference used to reverify access on the same browser and device. Clearing browser data may remove saved answers and the locally stored access reference.

Sharing

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service, including Stripe for payments, Google Analytics for usage analytics, Netlify for hosting and server functions, and email providers for support communications. Start Your Cause does not sell questionnaire answers.

Security and retention

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Browser-saved information remains until you clear it or use an available clear-data control. Payment and analytics providers retain information under their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear questionnaire data through the service's clear-data control or your browser settings. Browser privacy tools may limit analytics. For privacy questions, contact hello@startyourcause.org.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
