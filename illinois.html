The Illinois path, in plain language

1
Define your charitable purpose and organization.
Clarify what your nonprofit will do, who it will serve, and the activities it expects to conduct.
2
Build your initial board.
Illinois nonprofit corporations must have at least three directors. Your board will govern the organization and should be chosen intentionally.
3
Choose a name, registered agent, and Illinois registered office.
These are core pieces of the information needed for Illinois incorporation.
4
Prepare and file Articles of Incorporation.
Your organizing document creates the Illinois nonprofit corporation. Organizations seeking 501(c)(3) recognition should make sure their organizing language is appropriate for federal exemption.
5
Create bylaws and document board action.
Bylaws establish how the organization operates. Adopt them formally and keep written records of important board decisions.
6
Get an EIN and determine your federal application path.
Organizations seeking IRS recognition generally use Form 1023 or, when eligible, the streamlined Form 1023-EZ. Eligibility for 1023-EZ depends on the IRS Eligibility Worksheet—not just organization size.
7
Handle Illinois registrations and ongoing compliance.
Depending on your activities, additional Illinois tax and charitable-solicitation filings may apply. Nonprofits also have ongoing state and federal reporting obligations.
