# First Customer Revenue Sprint

## Objective
Get the first outside paying customer before expanding product scope.

## Primary funnel
Free 2-minute nonprofit readiness check -> Start Your Cause guided workflow -> paid subscription.

## Positioning
Start Your Cause is not merely an incorporation service. It helps an early-stage founder decide whether to form, organize the mission and governance, generate foundational documents, understand the 501(c)(3) path, and stay oriented toward ongoing compliance.

## 10-customer learning loop
For each early prospect/customer capture:
- acquisition source
- state
- stage (idea / forming / incorporated / seeking exemption / compliance)
- biggest question before purchase
- purchase or abandonment
- objection if known
- requested feature/help

Do not build a requested feature until the same problem appears repeatedly or it blocks payment/compliance.

## Initial acquisition experiments
1. Readiness assessment shared in founder/nonprofit communities where promotion is permitted.
2. Educational LinkedIn posts that answer one formation question and point to the assessment.
3. Referral outreach to bookkeepers, grant writers, nonprofit consultants and community organizations.
4. State-intent SEO pages that answer practical formation questions and route to the assessment.

## Partner pitch
Subject: Free nonprofit-startup resource for your clients

Start Your Cause helps early-stage founders understand the path from an idea to an organized nonprofit and 501(c)(3) readiness. We created a free two-minute readiness check that helps people determine whether they are ready to form or should do more groundwork first. If you regularly meet people who say “I want to start a nonprofit,” I’d be happy to make the resource available to them. There is no obligation to buy anything to use the assessment.

## Success metrics
- assessment starts
- assessment completions
- clicks from result to product
- checkout starts
- paid customers
- revenue
- acquisition source

North-star near-term metric: first outside paid customer.
