# Start Your Cause — legal-content accuracy review

Reviewed November 6, 2026. This is a source-based content audit, not attorney approval or a legal opinion.

## Finding

The software's functional tests did not establish legal accuracy. The review found material errors in federal annual-filing guidance, application preparation and several state-reference records. Confirmed errors were corrected in source. No jurisdiction has received a complete legal sign-off through this audit.

## Confirmed corrections

| Area | Original issue | Correction / current boundary |

## Matters NOT signed off

- All 56 jurisdictions have **not** been fully revalidated. Specific corrections in 12 jurisdiction records are not complete reviews of those 12 jurisdictions. Remaining records may contain outdated fees, agencies, exemptions, director minimums or other rules.
- Generic bylaws have not been validated for each state's director removal/election, voting-member rights, vacancies, officer combinations, notices, quorum, residency, indemnification or amendment rules. A visible limitation now identifies those review needs.
- The product assumes a board-governed nonprofit corporation without voting members. The intake does not resolve all other governance structures.
- Private-foundation drafts do not implement a complete section 508(e)/excise-tax and state-law analysis. They now carry an explicit review warning; this is not a completed private-foundation formation service.
- New Hampshire generally has separate five-incorporator and five-voting-director rules, with exceptions/waivers. The intake collects only one incorporator. The generated draft now explicitly says it is incomplete for that filing.
- Director addresses, multiple incorporator data/signatures, official forms and other jurisdiction-specific fields may need to be supplied separately.
- The eight-question 1023-EZ screener is preliminary only. It does not replace the complete IRS worksheet.
- State-specific sales/use-tax exemptions, multi-state fundraising, foreign qualification, UBIT, employment/payroll, gaming/raffles, scholarships, foreign activities, schools/churches/hospitals, public-disclosure rules and state legal-service restrictions were not exhaustively audited.
- Terms/privacy enforceability and consumer-law compliance were not given a legal opinion. No representation of attorney review should be made.

## Product disposition

Use “educational guidance and customizable drafts,” not “legally verified,” “attorney approved,” or “filing-ready in all 56 jurisdictions.” The organizing-purpose and dissolution framework, IRS application fees and preliminary nature of the EZ quiz have primary-source support, but that does not cure the unreviewed jurisdiction-specific or fact-specific issues above.

Automated regression tests protect the corrected dates and statements. Passing those tests is not legal sign-off. An attorney experienced with nonprofit formation should review the core templates and define supported jurisdictions/organization types before broad filing-ready claims are made.
