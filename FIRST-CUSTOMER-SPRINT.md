# Start Your Cause — First Paying Customer Sprint

## Goal
Get one paying customer who is not a friend or family member, then document the acquisition path so it can be repeated.

## Funnel
1. High-intent founder finds Illinois landing page or receives a useful direct link.
2. Founder uses the free Startup Path assessment.
3. Assessment explains likely next steps without claiming legal eligibility.
4. Founder enters the Start Your Cause guided intake.
5. Founder views pricing and starts checkout.
6. Paid subscriber receives the promised document-generation/access experience.

## Founding customer offer
Do not manufacture testimonials or fake scarcity. For the first 5 paying customers, offer one optional founder feedback/check-in after purchase. The product remains self-service; the check-in is for launch learning and confidence.

Suggested message:

> Starting a nonprofit in Illinois? I built Start Your Cause to make the formation process less overwhelming. The free startup-path tool gives you a plain-language snapshot of the steps you may need, and the paid product turns your answers into a guided workflow and customizable formation-document templates. I'm opening it to the first few founding customers now and personally reviewing their feedback as we launch.

## Prospect qualification
Prioritize people who have already decided to form an organization and are asking about incorporation, bylaws, boards, EIN sequencing, Form 1023/1023-EZ, charitable registration, or startup costs.

Do not spam communities that prohibit promotion. When participating in communities, answer the person's question first and mention Start Your Cause only when rules allow and the product is directly relevant.

## Partner targets
- nonprofit startup workshop organizers
- community foundations
- public libraries offering nonprofit education
- SCORE/nonprofit mentors
- grant writers and nonprofit consultants
- bookkeepers/accountants serving small nonprofits
- PTO/community group leaders

## Success criteria
Customer #1 = paid Stripe transaction from a customer outside the founder's friends/family, with acquisition source captured or reasonably attributable.

After customer #1, record:
- source
- landing page
- assessment completion
- objection/questions
- plan purchased
- time from first contact to purchase
- any point of confusion after purchase
